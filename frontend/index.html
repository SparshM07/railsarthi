Production AI Architecture & Operational Safeguards

RailsArthi employs two distinct LightGBM models engineered for specific operational horizons: real-time in-transit arrival cascading and holistic origin-to-destination delay simulation.

Production In-Transit

Real-Time Hop Delay Model (Champion V2)

13 Features

Predicts immediate next-station arrival delay and feeds the autoregressive 5-hop ETA cascading engine, adapting to in-transit recovery dynamics.

Production Feature Vector (13 Features)
Train number, current station, next station, current arrival delay, scheduled segment travel duration, historical segment stats (mean, median, std, count), previous station delay, day of week, month, is_weekend.
Ground Truth & Telemetry Alignment
Trained on chronological Indian Railways run logs; benchmarked against 17,317 indexed physical segment distributions and live RailRadar telemetry.
Validation Strategy
Chronological holdout validation on unseen Sep 25–30 journeys. Weather telemetry is ingested for contextual situational awareness but excluded from the production loss function to avoid data leakage.

Operational Safeguards & Downstream ETA Engine

Autoregressive 5-Hop Cascade

Recursive ML propagation across the next 5 stations, smoothly transitioning to flat propagation beyond hop 5 to prevent error runaway.

Monotonic Time Enforcement

Downstream arrival ETAs are mathematically guaranteed to never precede upstream arrivals or the current physical clock.

Midnight Day-Shift Protection

Seamlessly handles Indian Railways long-distance journeys that traverse midnight, preventing negative interval artifacts.

Zero-Downtime Provider Resilience

Automatic fallback to segment simulation when external feeds experience provider downtime or rate limiting.
